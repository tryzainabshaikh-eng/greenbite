# 🌱 GreenBite - Interactive Plant Care App

## ✨ What's New: Interactive Background

Your GreenBite app now has a **living, breathing background** with real plant images that respond to your movements!

### 🎨 Interactive Features

**Floating Plant Images**
- 8 real plant photographs float gently across the screen
- Each image has its own rotation speed and drift pattern
- Images fade in and out naturally for organic movement

**Mouse/Touch Parallax**
- Move your mouse (or touch on mobile) and watch the images shift
- Creates a beautiful 3D depth effect
- The background responds to your every movement

**Dynamic Radial Gradient**
- A soft green glow follows your cursor
- Adds warmth and focus to wherever you're looking
- Creates an atmospheric, immersive experience

**Smooth Animations**
- All movements are buttery smooth (60fps)
- Images rotate and drift continuously
- No jarring movements - everything flows naturally

### 🌿 How It Works

1. **Base Layer**: Cream-to-beige gradient background
2. **Floating Images**: 8 plant photos with parallax response
3. **Radial Gradient**: Follows your mouse/touch position
4. **Depth Overlay**: Subtle gradient for atmosphere

### 🎯 Try It Out

- **Desktop**: Move your mouse around the screen
- **Mobile**: Touch and drag to see the parallax effect
- Watch the plant images shift and rotate as you move
- Notice the green glow following your cursor

### 📱 Full App Flow

1. **Splash Screen** → Animated logo with tagline
2. **Login Screen** → Clean login or skip as guest
3. **Home Screen** → Features and "Start Identifying" button
4. **Camera Screen** → Demo mode to identify plants
5. **Result Screen** → Detailed care guide
6. **Journal Screen** → Track your plant's growth

### 🎨 Design Highlights

- **Real Images**: Actual plant photography from Unsplash
- **Fallback Colors**: Beautiful green gradients if images don't load
- **Performance**: Optimized for smooth 60fps animations
- **Responsive**: Works perfectly on desktop and mobile
- **Accessible**: High contrast text, clear navigation

This isn't just a static background - it's an **interactive experience** that:

- Feels alive and organic
- Enhances without distracting
